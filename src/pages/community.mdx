# OpenFGA Community

<div className="community-page">

## Slack (CNCF Community)

The OpenFGA community has a channel in the [CNCF](https://cncf.io) Slack.

If you don't have access to the CNCF Slack, you can request an invitation from the [CNCF Slack invite page](https://slack.cncf.io). Join the community in the [`#openfga` channel](https://cloud-native.slack.com/archives/C06G1NNH47N).

## GitHub Discussions

Use [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/openfga/discussions) to ask questions and submit product ideas.

## X (formerly Twitter)

Follow [@OpenFGA](https://twitter.com/OpenFGA) for the latest OpenFGA updates.

## YouTube

Subscribe to the [OpenFGA YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@OpenFGA) for videos and community meeting recordings.

## LinkedIn

Follow [OpenFGA on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/openfga/) for community highlights and fine-grained authorization updates.

## Mastodon

Follow [@openfga@mastodon.social](https://mastodon.social/@openfga) on the Fediverse.

## Monthly Community Meetings

The OpenFGA community meets on the second Thursday of every month at [11 AM Eastern Time](https://www.worldtimebuddy.com/?qm=1&lid=12,100,5,6,8&h=5&sln=11-12&hf=1).

- [Calendar and meeting details](https://zoom-lfx.platform.linuxfoundation.org/meetings/openfga)
- [Agenda](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1Y6rbD0xpGLVl-7CmeMgxi56_a0ibIQ_RojvWBbT9MZk/edit#)
- [Previous meeting recordings](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLUR5l-oTFZqUneyHz-h4WzaJssgxBXdxB)
- [Community meeting documentation](https://github.com/openfga/community/blob/main/community-meetings.md)
