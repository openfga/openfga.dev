OpenFGA Community

Slack (CNCF Community)​

The OpenFGA community has a channel in the CNCF Slack.

If you don't have access to the CNCF Slack, you can request an invitation from the CNCF Slack invite page. Join the community in the #openfga channel.

GitHub Discussions​

Use GitHub Discussions to ask questions and submit product ideas.

X (formerly Twitter)​

Follow @OpenFGA for the latest OpenFGA updates.

YouTube​

Subscribe to the OpenFGA YouTube channel for videos and community meeting recordings.

LinkedIn​

Follow OpenFGA on LinkedIn for community highlights and fine-grained authorization updates.

Mastodon​

Follow @openfga@mastodon.social on the Fediverse.

Monthly Community Meetings​

The OpenFGA community meets on the second Thursday of every month at 11 AM Eastern Time.
